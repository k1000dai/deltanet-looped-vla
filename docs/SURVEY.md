## 1. Executive Synthesis & Cross-Domain Positioning Matrix

Recent progress in **subquadratic sequence modeling** (Gated DeltaNet, Householder state-tracking, Log-Linear/Mixture-of-Memories) and **latent test-time compute** (Huginn, Ouro, LT2, MixerLoop) has evolved largely independently from **embodied VLA policies** (RoboMME, Dual-Stream Memory, RD-VLA, Looped Actor, RoboTTT, MemoryVLA). Cross-pollinating both literatures exposes three critical blind spots:

1. **VLA Memory Models Under-Utilize Both Write Algebra and Multi-Hop Readout (Primary-Source Verified):**  
   On `RoboMME` (`arXiv:2603.04639`, Table 2; 16 tasks), memory-free $\pi_{0.5}$ scores **17.93%**, recurrent `RMT` / `TTT` score **18.15–20.17%** / **21.96–22.35%**, and perceptual `FrameSamp+Modulator` reaches **44.51%** overall yet stalls at **25.11% on `Permanence`** (`VideoUnmaskSwap` **24.44%**, `ButtonUnmaskSwap` **18.22%**, whereas oracle-grounded `GroundSG Oracle` hits **93.31%** on `Permanence`!) and collapses from **45.3% $\to$ 19.3%** under cross-session `RoboMME-Interference` (`arXiv:2606.22338`, Table 2, $k_7$ distractors). Most tellingly, **Dual-Stream Memory** (`arXiv:2610.13262`, Table 6)—the only prior VLA incorporating Gated DeltaNet—reaches **49.22%** overall on `RoboMME` using a frozen 6-layer GDN over global DINOv3 `[CLS]` tokens + offline instance-mask-supervised keyframe retrieval, **yet scores only 27.75% on `Permanence` (regressing to 23.00% on `VideoUnmaskSwap`, 25.00% on `ButtonUnmaskSwap`, and dropping 53.3% $\to$ 40.0% on real-robot `Shell` cup swaps)**, explicitly concluding that *"failures under cup swaps expose persistent identity tracking as an unresolved requirement."*
2. **Write-Side Expressivity ($\beta \in (0, 2)$ & Read-Before-Write) vs. Read-Side Multi-Hop Looping Must Be Disentangled:**  
   LLM state-tracking theory (**Grazzi et al., ICLR 2025**; **DeltaProduct, Siems et al., 2025**) proves that standard GDN with $\beta_t \in (0, 1)$ has strictly positive transition eigenvalues and *mathematically cannot track permutations or parity*, whereas $\beta_t \in (0, 2)$ unlocks orthogonal Householder reflections capable of exact 1-step online slot swaps. Conversely, looped-transformer theory (**Saunshi et al., ICLR 2025**) shows that when relational chains must be resolved *retroactively at query time* (post-cue occlusion/reference) or verified against current observations, sequential depth-recurrent re-querying ($\mathbf{q}_t^{(0)} \to \mathbf{m}_t^{(0)} \to \mathbf{q}_t^{(1)} \dots$) provides an exponential parameter advantage over shallow single-pass readout.
3. **Looped VLAs (`RD-VLA`, `Looped Actor`) Achieve Strong Test-Time Scaling on Compact Backbones—But Are Strictly Markovian:**  
   `RD-VLA` demonstrates that a compact **0.5B VLM (`Qwen2.5-0.5B`)** with a Prelude–Recurrent Core–Coda action head reaches **93.0%** on LIBERO with $80\times$ lower latency than verbal CoT VLAs. However, because its loop only re-attends to the *current* frame, no amount of recurrent depth can recover an occluded target or disappeared visual cue.

## 2. Part I: LLM & Sequence Modeling Literature

### 2.1 Gated DeltaNet (Yang et al., ICLR 2025), KDA, and `fla` Kernel Constraints

#### Exact Mathematical Formulation
In *Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule* (Yang, Kautz, & Hatamizadeh, ICLR 2025), each attention head $h \in \{1, \dots, H\}$ maintains a matrix-valued associative state $\mathbf{S}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d_v \times d_k}$ (value-outer-key convention; $\mathbf{S}_t^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k \times d_v}$ in `fla` kernel memory layout). Given input hidden state $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$:

1. **Projections, Local Depthwise Short Convolution, and L2 Normalization:**
   $$\tilde{\mathbf{q}}_t, \tilde{\mathbf{k}}_t, \mathbf{v}_t = \operatorname{SiLU}\!\left(\operatorname{CausalConv1D}_{w=4}\!\left(\mathbf{W}_{qkv}\mathbf{x}_t\right)\right)$$
   $$\mathbf{q}_t = \frac{\tilde{\mathbf{q}}_t}{\|\tilde{\mathbf{q}}_t\|_2 + \epsilon} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k}, \qquad \mathbf{k}_t = \frac{\tilde{\mathbf{k}}_t}{\|\tilde{\mathbf{k}}_t\|_2 + \epsilon} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k}$$
   *Why L2 normalization is mandatory:* Because the state transition multiplies by $(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top)$, bounding $\|\mathbf{k}_t\|_2 = 1$ guarantees that the eigenvalues of $(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top)$ lie strictly in $\{1, 1 - \beta_t\}$, preventing exponential state explosion during long recurrences and chunkwise matrix inversions.

2. **Mamba-2 Style Log-Decay ($g_t$) and Write Gate ($\beta_t$):**
   $$g_t = -\exp(A_{\log}) \cdot \operatorname{softplus}\!\left(\mathbf{w}_a^\top \mathbf{x}_t + b_{\mathrm{dt}}\right) \in (-\infty, 0], \qquad \alpha_t = \exp(g_t) \in (0, 1]$$
   $$\beta_t = \sigma(\mathbf{w}_\beta^\top \mathbf{x}_t) \in (0, 1)$$
   where $A_{\log} \in \mathbb{R}^H$ (typically initialized uniformly in $\log([1, 16])$) and $b_{\mathrm{dt}} \in \mathbb{R}^H$ (initialized so $\operatorname{softplus}(b_{\mathrm{dt}}) \in [0.001, 0.1]$) set head-diverse base timescales.

3. **Online Gated Delta State Update & Readout:**
   Viewing $\mathbf{S}_{t-1}$ as a linear map $\mathbf{k} \mapsto \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{k}$ trained via decayed online L2 regression $\mathcal{L}_t(\mathbf{S}) = \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{S}\mathbf{k}_t - \mathbf{v}_t\|_2^2$:
   $$\mathbf{S}_t = \alpha_t \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\!\left(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top\right) + \beta_t \mathbf{v}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top = \alpha_t \mathbf{S}_{t-1} + \beta_t\!\left(\mathbf{v}_t - \alpha_t \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{k}_t\right)\mathbf{k}_t^\top$$
   $$\mathbf{o}_t = \operatorname{RMSNorm}(\mathbf{S}_t \mathbf{q}_t) \odot \operatorname{SiLU}(\mathbf{W}_g \mathbf{x}_t)$$

#### Hardware Parallelism (`chunk_gated_delta_rule` in `fla`) & Why Scalar $\alpha_t$ Matters
In `fla-org/flash-linear-attention`, sequences are split into chunks of length $C=64$. Using the **WY representation** (Bischof & Van Loan, 1987), products of generalized Householder matrices within a chunk are refactored via a $C \times C$ strictly lower-triangular matrix solve:
$$\mathbf{T} = \left(\mathbf{I}_C + \operatorname{strictTril}\!\left(\operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\beta}) \left(\boldsymbol{\Gamma} \odot \mathbf{K}\mathbf{K}^\top\right)\right)\right)^{-1}, \qquad \Gamma_{i,j} = \exp\!\left(\sum_{\tau=j+1}^{i} g_\tau\right) \text{ for } i > j$$
- **Constraint 1 — Scalar Per-Head Decay vs. Value-Vector Decay:**  
  If $\alpha_t \in (0,1)$ is **scalar per head**, $\boldsymbol{\Gamma} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times C}$ is shared across all $d_v$ value channels, so $\mathbf{T} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times C}$ is inverted **once per head per chunk** in SRAM. Conversely, if $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t \in (0,1)^{d_v}$ varies across *value* channels ($\mathbf{S}_t = \operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t)\mathbf{S}_{t-1}(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top) + \dots$), left-multiplication commutes with right-Householder updates algebraically, **but `chunk_gated_delta_rule` cannot factor a single $\mathbf{T}$**—requiring $d_v$ separate triangular solves per head!
- **Constraint 2 — Kimi Delta Attention (`chunk_kda`, Kimi Linear 2025):**  
  When channel-wise decay is needed, **KDA** places $\operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k \times d_k}$ on the **key** dimension:
  $$\mathbf{S}_t = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t)\!\left(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top\right) + \beta_t \mathbf{v}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top$$
  and uses a specialized **Diagonal-Plus-Low-Rank (DPLR)** chunkwise WY kernel (`chunk_kda` in `fla`) that absorbs channel-scaled keys into $\mathbf{K}_{\mathrm{scaled}}$ without $d_v\times$ inversion blowup.
- **Constraint 3 — The `bf16` Precision Trap Near $\alpha_t \to 1$:**  
  For retention half-life $T_{1/2}$ steps, $\alpha = 2^{-1/T_{1/2}}$ and $g = -\frac{\ln 2}{T_{1/2}}$:

  | Target Half-Life $T_{1/2}$ (steps) | Exact $\alpha = 2^{-1/T_{1/2}}$ | Exact $g = \log \alpha$ | Nearest `bf16` Value of $\alpha$ | Effective `bf16` Half-Life |
  | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
  | 20 | `0.965936` | `-3.4657e-2` | `0.964844` | 19.4 steps |
  | 100 | `0.993092` | `-6.9315e-3` | `0.992188` | 88.4 steps |
  | 300 | `0.997692` | `-2.3105e-3` | `0.996094` | 177.1 steps |
  | 500 (RoboMME mean ~480) | `0.998615` | `-1.3863e-3` | **`1.000000`** | **$\infty$ (no decay!)** |
  | 1,100 (RoboMME max) | `0.999370` | `-6.3013e-4` | **`1.000000`** | **$\infty$ (no decay!)** |
  | 3,000 (Multi-session) | `0.999769` | `-2.3105e-4` | **`1.000000`** | **$\infty$ (no decay!)** |

  Because `bf16` has only 7 mantissa bits (spacing $2^{-8} \approx 0.003906$ on $[0.5, 1)$), any $\alpha > 0.998047$ rounds to `1.0`. **All decay gates MUST be parameterized and cumulatively summed in `fp32` log-space ($g_t \in \mathtt{float32}$).**

- **Failure Mode — Counting Saturation Under Repeated Identical Writes:**  
  If an event occurs $n$ times with identical unit key $\mathbf{k}$, target value $\mathbf{v}$, $\alpha=1$, and constant $\beta \in (0,1)$, induction yields:
  $$\mathbf{S}_n \mathbf{k} = \left(1 - (1 - \beta)^n\right)\mathbf{v} \xrightarrow{n \to \infty} \mathbf{v}$$
  Unlike additive linear attention ($\mathbf{S}_n\mathbf{k} = n\mathbf{v}$), the delta rule erases its own accumulated value toward fixed point $\mathbf{v}$, causing **exponential saturation** for $n \ge 3$ unless the model uses (i) rotational/negative eigenvalues ($\beta \in (0,2)$ / multi-Householder), (ii) multi-layer read-before-write state updates ($\mathbf{v}_t = f(\mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{q}_t)$), or (iii) chunk-boundary write-back of incremented latent state.

### 2.2 Negative Eigenvalues (`Grazzi et al., ICLR 2025`) & `DeltaProduct` (`Siems et al., 2025`)

#### Spectral Bottleneck of $\beta_t \in (0, 1)$ (Grazzi et al., ICLR 2025)
In *"Unlocking State-Tracking in Linear RNNs Through Negative Eigenvalues"*, Grazzi et al. analyze the state-transition Jacobian $\mathbf{A}_t = \alpha_t(\mathbf{I} - \beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top)$ for $\|\mathbf{k}_t\|_2 = 1$:
- Along $\operatorname{span}(\mathbf{k}_t)^\perp$ ($d_k - 1$ dimensions): eigenvalue is $\lambda_\perp = \alpha_t > 0$.
- Along $\mathbf{k}_t$ ($1$ dimension): eigenvalue is $\lambda_\parallel = \alpha_t(1 - \beta_t)$.

When $\beta_t = \sigma(\cdot) \in (0, 1)$, $\lambda_\parallel \in (0, \alpha_t) > 0$. Finite-precision linear RNNs whose transition matrices have strictly non-negative real eigenvalues correspond algebraically to **aperiodic monoids** (circuit class $\mathsf{TC}^0$) and **provably cannot track even parity ($C_2$) or symmetric group permutations ($S_n, n \ge 2$)** over arbitrary horizons.

#### The $\beta_t \in (0, 2)$ Householder Fix
Simply rescaling the write gate by a factor of 2:
$$\beta_t = 2\,\sigma(\mathbf{w}_\beta^\top \mathbf{x}_t + b_\beta) \in (0, 2)$$
extends $\lambda_\parallel = \alpha_t(1 - \beta_t)$ to $(-\alpha_t, \alpha_t)$ with **zero extra parameters and zero extra FLOPs** (fully compatible with `fla`'s `chunk_gated_delta_rule`!).
- **Exact 1-Step Container Swap Construction:**  
  Suppose cups $A, B$ correspond to orthonormal key basis vectors $\mathbf{e}_A, \mathbf{e}_B \in \mathbb{R}^{d_k}$, and $\mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{e}_A = \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{ball}}, \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{e}_B = \mathbf{0}$. Setting $\alpha_t = 1$, $\beta_t = 2$, $\mathbf{v}_t = \mathbf{0}$, and difference key $\mathbf{k}_t = \frac{\mathbf{e}_A - \mathbf{e}_B}{\sqrt{2}}$ gives the exact orthogonal Householder reflection $\mathbf{H}_{AB} = \mathbf{I} - 2\mathbf{k}_t\mathbf{k}_t^\top$:
  $$\mathbf{S}_t \mathbf{e}_A = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{H}_{AB}\mathbf{e}_A = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{e}_B = \mathbf{0}, \qquad \mathbf{S}_t \mathbf{e}_B = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{H}_{AB}\mathbf{e}_B = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}\mathbf{e}_A = \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{ball}}$$
  Thus, **online transposition (`Swap(A, B)`) is representable in a single GDN write step once $\beta_t \in (0, 2)$!**

#### `DeltaProduct`: Multi-Householder Write-Side Looping (Siems et al., 2025)
A single Householder reflection ($\det = -1$ at $\beta=2$) is rank-1 per step: it cannot represent a pure $2\mathrm{D}$ rotation (such as $\bmod\,3$ cyclic counting or a 3-cycle $(A\,B\,C)$) in one step, because by the Cartan–Dieudonné theorem, an $SO(d)$ rotation in a plane requires a **product of 2 reflections**. `DeltaProduct` generalizes GDN by taking $n_h \ge 2$ virtual Householder steps per input token:
$$\mathbf{S}_t = \alpha_t \mathbf{S}_{t-1} \prod_{j=1}^{n_h} \left(\mathbf{I} - \beta_{t,j}\mathbf{k}_{t,j}\mathbf{k}_{t,j}^\top\right) + \sum_{j=1}^{n_h} \tilde{\mathbf{v}}_{t,j}\tilde{\mathbf{k}}_{t,j}^\top$$
- **Trade-off:** `DeltaProduct` expands the effective sequence length seen by the chunk kernel from $T$ to $n_h T$, multiplying write-side FLOPs and chunk-state memory by $n_h\times$.

> **Critical Insight for `DeltaLoop-VLA`:**  
> Any claim that Read-Side Looping is needed for swap/state tracking **would be confounded** if compared only against $\beta_t \in (0,1)$ single-layer GDN! We MUST include `Single-GDN (\beta \in (0,2))`, `2-Layer GDN (\beta \in (0,2))` (which adds parallelizable read-before-write), and `DeltaProduct` ($n_h$-step write loop) as first-class baselines, and explicitly distinguish:
> 1. **Online Known-Slot Tracking** (where write-side $\beta \in (0,2)$ / `DeltaProduct` excels), vs.
> 2. **Post-Cue / Retroactive Multi-Hop Relational Chaining & Cross-Observation Verification** (where the target query chain is unknown at write time, or visual-to-slot grounding requires iterative cross-checking between current pixels $\mathbf{z}_t$ and memory $\mathbf{S}_{t-1}$).

### 2.3 Looped / Recurrent-Depth LMs & Adaptive Compute

#### Theoretical Separation: Why Looped Depth Helps Multi-Step Reasoning (`Saunshi et al., ICLR 2025`)
In *"Reasoning with Latent Thoughts: On the Power of Looped Transformers"*, Saunshi et al. prove that for $p$-hop pointer chasing / induction and graph reachability:
- A $k$-layer block looped $L$ times matches the sequential reasoning depth of a $kL$-layer untied Transformer using $1/L$ the parameters, and **provably simulates $L$ steps of explicit Chain-of-Thought** in continuous latent space without emitting discrete tokens.
- Crucially, **parallel multi-head queries at depth $L=1$ cannot substitute for sequential depth $L \ge p$**: when hop $m+1$'s lookup key $\mathbf{q}^{(m+1)}$ depends on the value $\mathbf{m}^{(m)}$ retrieved at hop $m$, any $L=1$ circuit requires either $\Omega(|\mathcal{V}|^p)$ width/state pre-computation at write time or sequential depth $L \ge p$ at read time.

#### `Huginn` (`Geiping et al., 2025`) & `Ouro` (`2025`): Prelude–Core–Coda & Two-Stage Halting
- **Prelude–Recurrent Core–Coda Sandwich (`Huginn-0125`):**  
  Unrolling raw shared blocks from layer 0 causes representation collapse. `Huginn` establishes a 3-stage design:
  1. **Prelude $f_{\mathrm{pre}}$:** Maps raw inputs into latent workspace $\mathbf{e}_t$.
  2. **Recurrent Core $F_\theta$ with Persistent Input Injection:** At each step $l = 0, \dots, L-1$:
     $$\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t^{(l)} = \operatorname{RMSNorm}\!\left(\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{inj}}\!\left[\mathbf{h}_t^{(l)};\, \mathbf{e}_t\right] + \mathbf{b}_{\mathrm{step}}(l)\right), \qquad \mathbf{h}_t^{(l+1)} = F_\theta\!\left(\tilde{\mathbf{h}}_t^{(l)}\right)$$
     Persistent injection of $\mathbf{e}_t$ anchors the trajectory so deep unrolls ($L=16\text{--}32$) refine rather than forget the current observation. During training, `Huginn` runs $n-k$ iterations without gradients (`torch.no_grad()`) followed by $k=8$ TBPTT gradient steps.
  3. **Coda $f_{\mathrm{coda}}$:** Projects converged $\mathbf{h}_t^{(L)}$ to task output space.
- **Why Naive PonderNet Collapses & How `Ouro` Solves It:**  
  In **PonderNet** (Banino et al., 2021), step-wise halting hazard $\lambda_l \in (0,1)$ induces unconditional stopping probability $\tilde{p}_l = \lambda_l \prod_{j=0}^{l-1}(1-\lambda_j)$ (with remainder $\tilde{p}_{L_{\max}-1} = \prod_{j=0}^{L_{\max}-2}(1-\lambda_j)$), trained via:
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Ponder}} = \sum_{l=0}^{L_{\max}-1} \tilde{p}_l \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}^{(l)} + \beta_{\mathrm{KL}}\,\mathrm{KL}\!\left(\tilde{p} \,\|\, p_G(\lambda_p)\right), \qquad p_G(l) = \lambda_p(1-\lambda_p)^l$$
  **`Ouro`** (*"Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models"*, 2025) demonstrates a major failure mode of joint geometric-prior training: because early steps receive larger prior mass $p_G(0) = \lambda_p$ and converge faster early in training, the shared core $F_\theta$ starves deeper steps of gradient signal (**shallow/lazy depth collapse**). `Ouro` resolves this via a clean **Two-Stage Training Recipe**:
  - **Stage I (Uniform-Depth Backbone Training):** Train the policy across all depths $l \in \{0, \dots, L_{\max}-1\}$ with **uniform depth weighting** $\frac{1}{L_{\max}}\sum_l \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}^{(l)}$ (or uniform prior $\pi = \mathrm{Unif}(1, L_{\max})$), ensuring $F_\theta$ monotonically improves task loss with depth.
  - **Stage II (Frozen-Backbone Marginal-Gain Exit Gate):** Freeze $F_\theta$ and train only the tiny halting head $\lambda_l = \sigma(f_{\mathrm{halt}}(\cdot))$ via binary cross-entropy against marginal utility $w_l \in [0, 1]$ (indicating whether continuing past step $l$ meaningfully reduces loss):
    $$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{gate}}^{(l)} = -\left[w_l \log(1 - \lambda_l) + (1 - w_l)\log \lambda_l\right], \qquad w_l = \mathbb{I}\!\left[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}^{(l)} - \min_{j > l}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{task}}^{(j)} > \delta\right]$$

#### `LT2` vs. `MixerLoop`: Where Should Recurrent Compute Go?
- **`LT2` (*Linear-Time Looped Transformers*, 2025):** Replaces $O(T^2)$ full attention inside looped LM blocks with GDN/KDA (`LT2-linear`) or interleaved GDN + sparse/full attention (`LT2-hybrid`). Because `LT2` loops across the *entire token sequence* $[1..T]$ at every depth $l$, it updates $\mathbf{S}_t^{(l)}$ at every $(t, l)$ pair—achieving rank-$L$ iterative memory refinement at $O(L T)$ training/prefill cost.
- **`MixerLoop` (*Allocating Recurrent Compute in Looped Language Models*, 2026):** Introduces **Iterative Transport Rank (ITR)** to measure whether repeating a sub-layer exposes new cross-position information directions. `MixerLoop` shows that dense FFNs contribute minimal marginal ITR when repeated, whereas **looping the lightweight GDN/attention mixer while executing heavy FFNs once (or sparingly) cuts recurrent projection FLOPs by 45.9% while preserving core reasoning gains**.

### 2.4 Multi-Timescale & Hierarchical Linear Memory

When sequences span multiple orders of magnitude or suffer high-frequency distractor clutter, single-scale recurrences face a fundamental **plasticity–stability dilemma**:
1. **HGRN2 (Qin et al., ICML 2024) — Band/Lower-Bound Forget Gates:**  
   Unconstrained learnable forget gates across layers/banks suffer symmetry collapse into identical medium timescales. `HGRN2` enforces monotonic lower bounds $a_i \in [0, 1)$:
   $$\alpha_t^{(i)} = a_i + (1 - a_i)\,\sigma(\cdot) \in [a_i, 1)$$
   guaranteeing bank $i$ has minimum half-life $T_{1/2,\min}^{(i)} = -\ln 2 / \ln a_i$.
2. **Mixture-of-Memories (`MoM`, Du et al., ICLR 2025) — Sparse Write Routing Prevents Key-Collision Interference:**  
   Maintains $M$ independent memory matrices $\{\mathbf{S}_t^{(m)}\}_{m=1}^M$ and routes each token's write to only the top-$k$ ($k < M$) memories via $\mathbf{g}_t = \operatorname{Normalize}(\operatorname{TopK}(\operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf{x}_t \mathbf{W}_g)))$. Non-selected memories keep $\mathbf{S}_t^{(m)} = \mathbf{S}_{t-1}^{(m)}$ untouched ($\beta_t^{(m)} = 0$). **Key lesson:** Passive slow decay ($\alpha_t \to 1$) *alone* does NOT protect a long-term GDN bank if every step still applies $\beta_t \mathbf{k}_t \mathbf{k}_t^\top$ with overlapping keys! Protecting long-horizon memory requires **sparse write gating ($\beta_t^{(i)} \approx 0$ on routine steps, $\beta_t^{(i)} > 0$ only on salient events)**.
3. **Log-Linear Attention / Log-Linear GDN (Guo et al., 2025):**  
   Replaces a single state $\mathbf{S}_t$ with $\lceil \log_2 T \rceil + 1$ hierarchical Fenwick-tree buckets indexed by least-significant set bit (`lssb`), maintaining exact recent states at fine resolution and logarithmically compressed older states at $O(\log T)$ space and $O(T \log T)$ compute.
4. **Titans (Behrouz et al., 2025):**  
   Updates neural long-term memory via surprise momentum $\mathbf{M}_t = (1-\alpha_t)\mathbf{M}_{t-1} + \mathbf{S}_t$, $\mathbf{S}_t = \eta_t\mathbf{S}_{t-1} - \theta_t\nabla\ell(\mathbf{M}_{t-1}; \mathbf{x}_t)$, and formalizes three integration patterns: **MAC** (Memory-as-Context), **MAG** (Memory-as-Gate), and **MAL** (Memory-as-Layer)—directly mirroring `RoboMME`'s `{Context, Modulator, Expert}` taxonomy in VLA!

### 3.2 `Dual-Stream Memory for VLA Policies` (Wang et al., Magic-Lab & UCLA, `arXiv:2610.13262`)

- **Exact Primary-Source Architecture (Sec. III-B & III-C):**
  1. **Temporal Stream:** Processes front-view history at **stride 3** by projecting **DINOv3 `[CLS]` global tokens** (no spatial patches) to $d=512$ through a **6-layer standard Gated DeltaNet** ($\beta \in (0,1)$, Yang et al., 2025), outputting $\mathbf{c}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$ mapped to **1 policy temporal token**. Pretrained in Stage 1 with $\mathcal{L}_C = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{progress}} + \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{feat}} + 0.25\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{anchor}} + 0.09\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SIGReg}}$ (SmoothL1 execution progress + 4-layer reverse-action feature Retrospector + Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization), then **frozen** with auxiliary heads discarded in Stage 2.
  2. **Spatial Stream:** Computes patch-level LSE relevance logits $\ell_i = \log\sum_{n=1}^{256}\exp(\tau \mathbf{q}^\top \mathbf{K}_{in}) - \log 256$ over frozen SigLIP $16\times 16$ grids, retaining all frames with $\ell_i > 0.6$ ($16 m_t$ fused tokens) supervised offline by **simulator instance masks and annotated swap/keyframe intervals** ($\mathcal{L}_R = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{patch}} + \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{interval}}$).
- **Smoking-Gun Swap Failure Mode:** Even *with* simulator instance-mask keyframe supervision, `Dual-Stream` scores only **27.75%** on `Permanence` (**regressing** on `VideoUnmaskSwap` from 24.44% $\to$ **23.00%**, reaching only **25.00%** on `ButtonUnmaskSwap`, and dropping from 53.3% $\to$ **40.0%** on real-robot `Shell` cup swaps!). As the authors explicitly conclude in their abstract: *"failures under cup swaps expose persistent identity tracking as an unresolved requirement."* This occurs because (i) their GDN sees only global `[CLS]` vectors with $\beta \in (0,1)$ and is frozen as a task-agnostic progress scalar, and (ii) single-pass ($L=1$) cross-attention over static keyframes cannot chain multi-step container permutations!

### 3.3 `RD-VLA` & `Looped Actor`: Latent Test-Time Compute in Robotics

- **`RD-VLA` (*Recurrent-Depth VLA*, 2025/2026):**
  - Instantiates `Huginn`-style **Prelude ($P_\phi$) $\to$ Weight-Tied Recurrent Core ($R_\theta$) $\to$ Coda ($C_\psi$)** as the action head atop a **compact 0.5B VLM (`Qwen2.5-0.5B` + LoRA)**:
    $$\mathbf{x}_l = \operatorname{RMSNorm}\!\left(\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{adapt}}\!\left[\mathbf{h}_{l-1};\, \mathbf{h}_{\mathrm{pre}}\right]\right), \qquad \mathbf{h}_l = R_\theta\!\left(\mathbf{x}_l,\, [\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{VLM}};\, \mathbf{s}_{\mathrm{prop}}]\right)$$
  - Trained with Poisson-lognormal depth sampling and **TBPTT window $d = 8$**; halts adaptively at test time up to $L_{\max}=32$ when consecutive latent scratchpad states converge (cosine similarity / L2 delta threshold).
  - Achieves **93.0%** (fixed depth) and **92.5%** (adaptive depth) on **LIBERO**, **3.39** average chain length on **CALVIN (`ABC -> D`)**, and an **$80\times$ inference speedup** over autoregressive verbal CoT VLAs.
- **`Looped Actor` (*Depth-Recurrent Reasoning Models for RL*, 2025/2026):**
  - Iterates a weight-tied policy core toward fixed point $\mathbf{z}^\star = f_\theta(\mathbf{z}^\star; \mathbf{x})$, matching untied policies with **$16\times$ more parameters** and halting via policy distribution stability $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_{\ell-1} \| \pi_\ell) < \tau$.
  - **Adaptation Note for Flow-Matching VLAs:** Evaluating velocity-field convergence $\|v_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{\tau_0}, \tau_0 \mid \mathbf{c}_t^{(l+1)}) - v_\theta(\mathbf{x}_{\tau_0}, \tau_0 \mid \mathbf{c}_t^{(l)})\|_2$ requires running the 300M Action Expert per loop step (~30–60 GFLOPs vs ~3 GFLOPs for the loop core). Hence, zero-AE-overhead **Coda latent convergence** $\|\mathbf{c}_t^{(l+1)} - \mathbf{c}_t^{(l)}\|_2 < \tau_c$ and **Stage II learned exit gates** must be benchmarked alongside velocity convergence in wall-clock latency accounting!

### 3.4 `RoboTTT` (2026) & `MemoryVLA` (Shi et al., 2025)

- **`RoboTTT`:** Compresses up to **8,000 visuomotor steps** into fast-weight matrices updated via online self-supervised reconstruction gradients at test time, enabling one-shot video imitation and 10-stage assembly. However, TTT inner-loop gradient updates require materialized forward-backward passes per chunk and perform **1-shot query readout ($L=1$)** per action chunk.
- **`MemoryVLA`:** Maintains short-term working memory tokens $\mathcal{W}_t = \operatorname{VLM}(o_t)$ alongside a hippocampal **Perceptual-Cognitive Memory Bank** $\mathcal{M}_t$ updated via similarity-based redundancy merging, feeding a Diffusion Action Expert. While effective against redundant static frames, similarity merging struggles when visually identical objects/cups undergo state swaps (merging distinct swap events!) and still performs single-pass retrieval.

## 4. Concrete Architectural Lessons & Implementation Rules for `DeltaLoop-VLA` (v2.2)

1. **Per-Frame (First-Slot Only) `fp32` Log-Decay + $\beta \in (0, 2)$ + $d_k = 128$ Capacity:**  
   When writing $M_\tau \in \{N_w, N_w+1\}$ slots per frame into `chunk_gated_delta_rule`, apply $g_\tau^{(h)} < 0$ **only on slot $s=1$ and set $g_{\tau, s>1}^{(h)} = 0.0$** so 1 frame incurs exact $\alpha_\tau$ decay rather than $\alpha_\tau^{M_\tau}$. Use `fla`-standard $d_k = d_v = 128$ ($H=8$, 512 KB/env) + `fp32` L2-normalized keys + $\beta \in (0,2)$ Householder gates + temporal (never across unordered intra-frame slots!) short conv.
2. **Disentangle Write Expressivity, Untied Depth, Parallel Queries, and Weight-Tied Read Loops (`H1a` vs. `H1b`):**  
   Compare against `Single-GDN β∈(0,2)`, `2-Layer GDN β∈(0,2)`, `Write-Loop (DeltaProduct)`, **`Untied L-Layer Deep Readout`**, and **query-count-matched `Parallel Multi-Query (K = N_q · L)`**.
3. **Video-Demo-Preserving Chunk-Horizon Exclusion + Depth-Consistent Segment-Sequential Write-Back:**  
   Set `cutoff_idx = max(exec_start_idx, step_idx - Δ_excl)` ($\Delta_{\mathrm{excl}} = H_{\mathrm{exec}} = 16$) so video demonstration prefix frames (`0 .. exec_start_idx - 1`) remain 100% preserved on chunk 0 while recent execution frames cannot self-retrieve; sample write-back depth $l_{\mathrm{wb}} \sim \mathrm{Uniform}\{0..L_{\max}-1\}$ in Stage I and bound dual-axis TBPTT ($d_{\mathrm{grad}}=4$ loop steps, $K_{\mathrm{wb}}=4$ boundaries).
4. **Flamingo-Style Zero-Init $\tanh$-Gated Cross-Attention + `QK-RMSNorm` Stability:**  
   Preserve exact step-0 equivalence to $\pi_{0.5}$ by adding Coda spatial tokens via a dedicated $\tanh(\gamma_{\mathrm{ctx}})$-gated Cross-Attention branch ($\gamma_{\mathrm{ctx}}=0$) alongside zero-init additive `Pi05AdaRMSNorm` modulation, with `QK-RMSNorm` inside attention blocks to prevent `bf16` logit spikes under adaptive norm modulation and separate gradient-norm clipping for `Write` vs. `Loop` parameters.
5. **Delta-Consistent Occupancy $\mathbf{s}_{\mathrm{occ}}$, Damped Readout Norm, & Winner's-Curse-Free CRN Gate:**  
   Track key occupancy via $1 \times d_k$ delta rule $\mathbf{s}_{\mathrm{occ}, u} = \alpha_u \mathbf{s}_{\mathrm{occ}, u-1}(\mathbf{I} - \min(\beta_u,1)\mathbf{k}_u\mathbf{k}_u^\top) + \min(\beta_u,1)\mathbf{k}_u^\top$, damp empty-state readout normalization ($\epsilon_{\mathrm{read}}=0.1$, zero-init $\mathbf{W}_o$), and compute Stage II gate labels using **$M_{\mathrm{CRN}}=8$-averaged** Flow-Matching losses $\bar{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{FM}}^{(l)} - \bar{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{FM}}^{(L_{\max}-1)} > \delta$.
6. **Occam's-Razor Long-Horizon Memory (Unified `fp32` Diverse Log-Decay / 2-Layer Residual GDN Primary; Conditional Sparse $\beta$-Gate Only on Interference):**  
   Use unified multi-head `fp32` diverse log-decay or 2-layer residual GDN as the primary long-horizon memory (avoiding MoE softmax scale-routing collapse), reserving sparse $\beta$-gated consolidation strictly for verified cross-session interference (`Gate 3`).

## 5. Cycle 1 Theoretical Follow-Up: Explaining the Synthetic Discoveries

Cycle 1 (`87 runs = 3 seeds × all variants`, [results/synthetic/SUMMARY_CYCLE1.md](file:///home/kohei/codes/ghq/github.com/k1000dai/deltanet-looped-vla/results/synthetic/SUMMARY_CYCLE1.md)) surfaced three sharp empirical phenomena that directly refine both our theoretical framing and Cycle 2 VLA architecture:

### 5.1 Why Untied Depth ($L=6$) Fails `Forward`-Causal Composition While Weight-Tied `DeltaLoop` Extrapolates Zero-Shot
- **Empirical Phenomenon (`Task 1A-Retro`):** Every single-pass baseline (`Single-GDN β∈(0,2)` `60.3%`, `2-Layer GDN` `60.3%`, `Write-Loop` `59.8%`, `Parallel Multi-Query` `60.0%`) **and** the $3.7\times$-larger **`6-Layer Untied Deep Readout` (`1.63M` params, `61.4 ± 1.5%`)** solve `Reverse` arrival order (`93.7–95.5%`, where `Box -> Zone` precedes `Apple -> Box`), **yet collapse to chance (`17.1–21.4%`) on physically realistic `Forward` (`Apple -> Box` at $t_1$, then closed `Box -> Zone` at $t_2 > t_1$) and `Mixed` (`15.9–19.1%`) causal orders**. By contrast, weight-tied **`DeltaLoop` (`436K` params)** achieves **`97.9 ± 0.8%` (`b1`) / `96.3 ± 2.1%` (`b2`) ID overall**, **`97.1%` / `94.8%` on `Forward`**, and **zero-shot OOD $k=5$ extrapolation of `85.0 ± 2.9%` / `82.3 ± 5.8%`** (vs `39.0–41.9%` untied).
- **Write-Time Causal Asymmetry & $\mathsf{TC}^0 \subsetneq \mathsf{NC}^1$ (`Merrill, Petty & Sabharwal, ICML 2024`):**  
  In `Reverse` order, when `Apple -> Box` arrives at $t_2 > t_1$, `Box -> Zone` is already stored in $\mathbf{S}_{t_2-1}$, so a multi-layer or write-loop encoder can read `Zone` at $t_2$ and bind `Apple -> Zone` on write. In physical `Forward` manipulation (`Apple` placed in `Box` at $t_1$, closed `Box` moved to `Zone` at $t_2 > t_1$), the update at $t_2$,
  $$\mathbf{S}_{t_2} = \alpha_{t_2}\mathbf{S}_{t_2-1}\!\left(\mathbf{I} - \beta_{t_2}\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{Box}}\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{Box}}^\top\right) + \beta_{t_2}\mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{Zone}}\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{Box}}^\top,$$
  modifies key $\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{Box}}$ *without knowing which historical keys $\mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{Apple}}$ point to value $\mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{Box}}$* (since $\mathbf{S}$ indexes by key, not value). Consequently, `Forward` chains cannot be pre-composed at write time in $O(1)$ associative state and strictly require sequential post-cue pointer dereferencing. Simultaneously, on `Task 1A-Online` ($S_8$ cup swaps), `Single-GDN β∈(0,1)` (`25.1%` ID / `4.5%` OOD) and `4L Softmax Transformer` (`15.6%` ID / `4.5%` OOD) hit the **$\mathsf{TC}^0$ circuit-complexity barrier** proved by **Merrill et al. (ICML 2024, *"The Illusion of State in State-Space Models"*)**, whereas $\beta \in (0,2)$ Householder reflections cross into $\mathsf{NC}^1$ (`Grazzi et al., ICLR 2025`), reaching **`100.0 ± 0.0%` ID & OOD ($k=5,6$)**.
- **Untied Latent Two-Hop Breakdown (`Yang et al., ACL 2024`) vs. Weight-Tied Shift-Invariant Operators (`Giannou et al., ICML 2023`; `Saunshi et al., ICLR 2025`):**  
  Why did even a 6-layer untied readout (`1.63M` params) fail `Forward` chains (`21.4%`)? **Yang et al. (ACL 2024, *"Do Large Language Models Latently Perform Multi-Hop Reasoning?"*)** show that standard untied Transformer layers reliably resolve the bridge entity $e_2$ in `(e1, r1, e2)` yet struggle to latently compose the second hop `(e2, r2, e3)` without explicit CoT supervision because each untied layer $\ell$ must independently learn its own subspace alignment and dereferencing map $F_{\theta_\ell}$ from noisy, attenuated gradients at depth $\ell$. Conversely, **Giannou et al. (ICML 2023, *"Looped Transformers as Programmable Computers"*)** and **Saunshi et al. (ICLR 2025)** prove that **weight tying** enforces a **stationary, shift-invariant transition operator** $\mathbf{h}^{(l+1)} = F_\theta(\mathbf{h}^{(l)}, \mathbf{S}_{\mathrm{past}})$: strong supervision on $k=1,2$ hops directly trains the *exact same* dereferencing operator $F_\theta$ applied at $l=3,4$ (`94.8–97.1%`) and zero-shot OOD $l=5,6$ (`82.3–85.0%`), confirmed by QK-Hop Top-1 alignment ($l=1 \to 84.9\%$ Hop-1, $l=2 \to 72.2\%$ Hop-2, $l=3 \to 38.1\%$ Hop-3).
- **Why Marginal-Benefit Gates Beat Latent L2 Convergence (`+25.4` to `+35.9` pts):**  
  Intermediate pointer states $\mathbf{h}^{(1)}, \mathbf{h}^{(2)}$ lie on the same normalized entity manifold, causing L2 step deltas $\|\mathbf{h}^{(l+1)} - \mathbf{h}^{(l)}\|_2$ (`RD-VLA` stopping rule) to contract prematurely (`61.9–70.4%`), whereas `Ouro`-style Stage-II marginal-loss gates (`95.8–97.8%` @ `2.62–2.65` loops, `-56%` FLOPs) explicitly detect whether another dereferencing hop reduces prediction loss.

### 5.2 Multi-Query ($N_q > 1$) Inverse Key-Value Lookup & Chunk-Boundary Latent Write-Back
- **Inverse Lookup Subspace Probing (`Arora et al., ICLR 2024, Zoology / MQAR`):**  
  In `Task 1A-Online`, swaps only reference cup locations (`Swap(Cup_i, Cup_j)`), so $\mathbf{S}_T \approx \sum_{c=1}^8 \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{obj}(c)} \mathbf{k}_{\mathrm{cup}(c)}^\top$ maps **`Cup (key) -> Obj (value)`**. Querying **`Where is Obj_m?`** is therefore an *inverse value-to-key lookup*. With only $N_q = 1$ query per loop step, $l=0$ suffers gradient competition between issuing a static 8-cup superposition probe and learning sequential dereferencing (`99.7%` on seed 0 vs `63.6 ± 29.1%` 3-seed mean). Consistent with **Arora et al. (ICLR 2024, *Zoology*)** on Multi-Query Associative Recall (`MQAR`), providing **$N_q = 4$ query tokens $\times H$ heads per loop step** gives $l=0$ $4H$ parallel container-slot probes to inspect candidate containers simultaneously, freeing $l \ge 1$ for iterative disambiguation and multi-hop chaining.
- **Block/Chunk-Boundary Working-Memory Write-Back (`Hutchins et al., NeurIPS 2022`; `ReMem-VLA` & `RePlanMem-VLA`, 2026):**  
  **Block-Recurrent Transformers** (`Hutchins et al., NeurIPS 2022`) establish that parallel intra-block ingestion coupled with gated recurrent summary-slot write-back at block boundaries preserves cross-block reasoning in $O(1)$ state. In embodied manipulation, **`ReMem-VLA`** (dual-level recurrent queries across frame and chunk granularities) and **`RePlanMem-VLA`** (replanning-triggered online memory writes) similarly show that persisting distilled working-memory states across action chunks avoids re-deriving transient visual events from scratch—directly supporting `DeltaLoop-VLA`'s chunk-boundary write-back of $\bar{\mathbf{c}}_t$ (`m6b_deltaloop_wb`).

### 5.3 Implicit Multi-Timescale Consolidation vs. Softmax Scale-Routing Collapse (`Task 1B`)

- **Multi-hop `smolvla_m7_deltaloop_adaptive` ($L_t \in \{1..4\}$, `N_c = 4`):** Scores **`4.25%` (`17/400`, `H1a: p = 0.0136` unadjusted, non-significant after Holm correction)**, with `6/100 = 6.0%` on `Permanence` (`VideoUnmaskSwap 2/25 = 8.0%`, `VideoUnmask 3/25 = 12.0%`) and `9/100 = 9.0%` on `Imitation` (`PatternLock 5/25 = 20.0%`, `RouteStick 4/25 = 16.0%`), but drops on `Reference` (`2/100 = 2.0%` vs `9/100 = 9.0%` on `M5`) and trails `FrameSamp-32` (`26/400 = 6.50%`) on `RouteStick` (`4/25` vs `10/25`) and `VideoPlaceButton` (`0/25` vs `5/25`).

Synthesizing Linear-RNN capacity theory and multimodal resampler literature motivates a mechanistic hypothesis for this `M5 > M7` gap: **the `520.0 KB` 2-layer GDN state already stores `Reference` landmarks (`M5 = 9.0%`, `Probe 2 = 93.2%`), whereas training `N_c = 4` Coda tokens across random depths $l \in \{0..3\}$ introduces readout-token bandwidth and register contention (`N_c = 4` vs. `32`).**

### 8.1 LLM & Linear-RNN Theory: Matrix Write Capacity vs. Low-Rank Readout & Register Contention
1. **Refuting Write-State Exhaustion (`Jelassi et al., ICML 2024` vs. `Yang et al., ICLR 2025`):**  
   **Jelassi et al. (ICML 2024, *"Repeat After Me: Transformers are Better than State Space Models at Copying"*)** prove that Generalized State Space Models (GSSMs) with fixed state dimension $D_{\mathrm{state}}$ hit an information-theoretic write bottleneck when copying strings exceeding $O(D_{\mathrm{state}})$ bits. However, in 2-layer Gated DeltaNet (`Yang et al., ICLR 2025`), each layer maintains $H = 8$ matrix-valued fast-weight heads $\mathbf{S}_u^{(h)} \in \mathbb{R}^{128 \times 128}$ (`2 × 8 × 128 = 2,048` key dimensions, `532,480 B` including occupancy), updated via orthogonalizing delta rules. Both `Probe 2` (`91.5%–93.8%` count/demo retention, `99.1%` `PickHighlight`) and `smolvla_m5_gdn2l_l1`'s closed-loop **`9.0%` `Reference` SR (`= FrameSamp-32`)** prove empirically that all required video-demonstration landmarks *are* faithfully stored in $\mathbf{S}_{\mathrm{past}, t}$.
2. **The Rank-$N_c$ Readout Interface Bottleneck (`Arora et al., ICLR 2024, Zoology`):**  
   **Arora et al. (ICLR 2024, *Zoology*)** demonstrate on Multi-Query Associative Recall (`MQAR`) that associative recall depends jointly on *state capacity* ($d_k d_v$) and *query readout bandwidth*. Regardless of how many object-location bindings $\sum_{i=1}^M \mathbf{v}_i \mathbf{k}_i^\top$ are stored inside $\mathbf{S}_{\mathrm{past}, t} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times d_v \times d_k}$, the Coda interface transmitted to the 8 odd `SmolVLA` Action Expert layers (`SmolVLAExpertMemoryHook`, `d_mem = 384`) is a $N_c \times d_{\mathrm{mem}}$ matrix:
   $$\mathbf{C}_t^{(L_t)} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_c \times 384}, \qquad \operatorname{rank}\!\left(\mathbf{C}_t^{(L_t)}\right) \le N_c.$$
   In Cycle 3B Wave-1, `smolvla_m2_framesamp` exposed **`32` buffer tokens (`32 × 384 = 12,288` floats)** to Expert cross-attention, whereas `smolvla_m7_deltaloop` squeezed its entire `520.0 KB` state through only **`N_c = 4` Coda tokens (`4 × 384 = 1,536` floats — an `8×` narrower cross-attention bottleneck)**! On multi-waypoint geometric tasks (`RouteStick` with 4–6 stick-routing waypoints around pegs, `VideoPlaceOrder` with 3–4 ordered placements, `VideoPlaceButton`), 4 tokens barely span the simultaneous obstacle + waypoint manifold needed by a 20-step continuous trajectory generator.
3. **Readout Register Contention / "Token Recycling" Across Hops (`Darcet et al., ICLR 2024 Oral`):**  
   **Darcet et al. (ICLR 2024, *"Vision Transformers Need Registers"*)** discovered that when a Transformer lacks dedicated scratchpad register tokens, iterative layers hijack spatial patch tokens to store intermediate global computations ("token recycling"), corrupting local spatial coordinates. An exact temporal analogue occurs in `smolvla_m7_deltaloop` at $N_c = 4$:
   - At $L = 1$ (`smolvla_m5_gdn2l_l1`), all $N_c = 4$ tokens specialize purely as **static landmark carriers** (`Reference = 9.0%`).
   - Under uniform multi-depth training ($l \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$) in `smolvla_m7_deltaloop`, the **same 4 tokens** are forced to simultaneously act as **(a) persistent spatial landmark outputs** for Expert cross-attention at $l=0$ *and* **(b) mutable pointer-chasing registers** whose embeddings shift at $l=1,2,3$ to generate next-hop queries $\mathbf{Q}_t^{(l)} = W_Q(\operatorname{RMSNorm}(\mathbf{C}_t^{(l)}) + \mathbf{e}_{\mathrm{step}}[l])$. Mutating $\mathbf{C}_t^{(l)}$ across hops resolves occluded container swaps (`Permanence: 3.0% -> 6.0%`, `PatternLock: 12.0% -> 20.0%`), but overwrites static demo landmarks when $N_c = 4$ has no spare register slots!

### 8.2 VLA & Multimodal Resampler Precedents (`Flamingo`, `BLIP-2`, `Token Turing Machines`) & The `U7` Prescription
- **Multimodal Query Token Scaling (`Alayrac et al., NeurIPS 2022`; `Li et al., ICML 2023`; `Ryoo et al., CVPR 2023`):**  
  Across vision-language and sequential robot architectures, cross-attention bottlenecks universally require **`16` to `64` query tokens** to avoid multi-object crowding: **Flamingo**'s `Perceiver Resampler` uses **`64` latent queries** (`Alayrac et al., 2022`), **BLIP-2**'s `Q-Former` uses **`32` learned queries** (`Li et al., 2023`), `SmolVLA` itself uses **`64` PixelShuffle tokens per view** (`Shukor et al., 2025`), and **Token Turing Machines (`TTM`, `Ryoo et al., CVPR 2023`)** explicitly decouple memory storage size ($m \approx 96$ tokens) from the read/controller bottleneck ($r = 16$ tokens) for vision-based robot policy learning, showing sharp degradation when $r$ drops below $8$–$16$.
- **Cycle 4A Hypothesis `U7` (`smolvla_m7_deltaloop_q16`: Homogeneous `N_c = 4 \to 16` at Strict `520.0 KB` Iso-State):**  
  Increasing `num_coda_tokens` from `4` (`1,536` floats) to **`16` (`6,144` floats)** adds only **`4,608` query-embedding parameters (`+0.0033%` of `141.18M` Stage-I trainable params)** and **zero bytes (`0 B`) to the `532,480 B` (`520.0 KB`) recurrent GDN state**, directly testing whether expanding readout bandwidth recovers `Reference` landmarks.

### 8.3 Cycle 4A (`U7`) & Cycle 4B (`U8`) Empirical Dissection: Single-Pass `M5` Dominance, Softmax Slot Dilution, & Anchor-Shortcut Halting
Evaluating Cycle 4A **`U7` (`smolvla_m7_deltaloop_q16_adaptive`, homogeneous `N_c = 16`)** and Cycle 4B **`U8` (`smolvla_m7_deltaloop_anchor`, `4+4` Anchor+Pointer)** across `N = 400` official `test` episodes (`16` tasks) and `82` mechanistic probes ([`SUMMARY_CYCLE3B.md`](file:///home/kohei/codes/ghq/github.com/k1000dai/deltanet-looped-vla/results/robomme/cycle3b_smolvla/SUMMARY_CYCLE3B.md)) clarifies why **dedicated single-pass 2L GDN (`smolvla_m5_gdn2l_l1`, `5.25%` = `21/400`, Holm-significant `p = 0.0012` vs `m0`) remains superior to all multi-hop Coda variants (`4.00%–4.50%`) in closed-loop RoboMME**:
1. **Multi-Landmark Training/Probe Gains in `U7` (`N_c = 16`):** Scaling `N_c: 4 \to 16` achieved the **lowest training `fm_loss` (`0.03155 \pm 0.00052`)**, **#1 overall zero-shot `U4` grounding (`4.36 px`, vs `FrameSamp-32` `4.40 px`)**, and a `10/10` Coda `<x, y>` Ridge sweep (`6.11 px` vs `14.68 px`). In closed-loop rollouts (`16/400 = 4.00%`, `H8a vs m0: +2.50%, two-sided p = 0.0246` unadjusted, non-significant after Holm correction), `U7` solved episodes in all `4/4` suites (`Reference: 5/100 = 5.0%`, `VideoPlaceOrder: 3/25 = 12.0%`), alongside a structured `CRNHaltingGate` phase profile (`Chunk-0 L_t = 1.951` post-demo deliberation vs `StopCube = 1.004`, `Mean L_t = 1.423`).
2. **Un-Partitioned Softmax & Workspace Dilution (`Veličković et al., ICLR 2025`; `Locatello et al., NeurIPS 2020`; `Goyal et al., ICLR 2022`):**  
   Despite improving offline grounding, making all `16` Coda slots *homogeneously mutable* inside `Recurrent Core`, `Readout`, and `FiLM = mean(C_t)` reduced `Permanence` (`2/100 = 2.0%` vs `6/100 = 6.0%` in `M7`) and `PatternLock` (`2/25 = 8.0%` vs `5/25 = 20.0%`, `H8b: p = 0.9266` NS), remaining below single-pass `M5` (`5.25%`):
   - **Softmax Attention Dispersion (`Veličković et al., ICLR 2025`):** Standard Softmax weights $\alpha_j = \exp(s_j)/\sum_{m=1}^{N_c}\exp(s_m)$ disperse mass as $N_c$ grows when only 1–2 occluded containers carry task-defining identity.
   - **Competitive Slot Over-Fragmentation (`Locatello et al., NeurIPS 2020`; `Goyal et al., ICLR 2022`):** Scarcity (`N_c = 4`) enforces compact entity binding, whereas over-provisioning homogeneous mutable slots (`N_c = 16`) dilutes pooled FiLM modulation $\frac{1}{N_c}\sum_{j=1}^{N_c}\mathbf{c}_{t,j}^{(l)}$.
   - **Explicit Role Partitioning in `Darcet et al. (ICLR 2024)`:** ViT Registers partition tokens into **spatial anchor tokens** and **dedicated scratchpad `[REG]` tokens** rather than mutating all tokens uniformly.
3. **Cycle 4B Prescription `U8` (`smolvla_m7_deltaloop_anchor`) & Exploratory "Anchor-Shortcut Halting" Observation (`Kaya et al., ICML 2019`; `Geirhos et al., 2020`; `Schuster et al., NeurIPS 2022`):**  
   `U8` keeps `Prelude`, `Core`, `Readout1/2`, `Coda`, `SubgoalHead`, and `CRNHaltingGate` at **`N_c = 4`** (`520.0 KB` exact iso-state) and passes a role-partitioned `8`-token tensor $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{exp}, t}^{(l)} = [\mathbf{C}_t^{(0)} + \mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{anc}};\, \mathbf{C}_t^{(l)} + \mathbf{r}_{\mathrm{ptr}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{8 \times 384}$ (`+768` params) to `SmolVLAExpertMemoryHook`:
   - **Strong Offline Probe Gains vs. `< 10%` Closed-Loop Floor:** Offline, `U8` achieves the lowest `aux_loss` (`0.004434`) & `ground_loss` (`0.017067`) across all 14 checkpoints, `2.5×` stronger `l=1 \to 4` Coda pointer sharpening (`-0.409 px` vs `M7` `-0.162 px`), `93.52%` `S_past` count retention, and `2.83 px` waypoint zero-shot `U4` error (`+0.14 px` $k=3$ drift). In closed loop, however, immutable Hop-0 anchors flatten teacher-forced multi-depth `fm_loss` differences ($\delta_{\mathrm{cal}} = 1.64\times 10^{-4}$), causing `CRNHaltingGate` to halt at **`L = 1` on `96.1%` of chunks (`Mean L_t = 1.039`, `12/400 = 3.00%` SR, `H9a: p = 0.1626` NS)**. Evaluating `smolvla_m7_deltaloop_anchor_L2` (`Fixed L=2` on identical weights) yields `15/400 = 3.75%` (`RouteStick: 2/25 \to 5/25`, `PickHighlight: 1/25 \to 2/25`, `VideoUnmask: 2/25 \to 3/25`); while directionally consistent with Hop-1 shortcut suppression, **both `H9f` (`+0.75%`, two-sided `p = 0.4832` NS) and `H9g` vs `M0` (`+2.25%`, two-sided `p = 0.0612` NS; one-sided `p_1 = 0.0306`) are statistically non-significant** under the `< 10%` success-rate floor.

### 8.4 Cross-Regime Halting Synthesis & Multi-Seed Statistical Audit (`Bai et al., NeurIPS 2019`)
Evaluating **`smolvla_m7_deltaloop_coda_conv` (`Latent L2 Convergence`, $\|\mathbf{C}_t^{(l)} - \mathbf{C}_t^{(l-1)}\|_2 < \tau = 0.06$, calibrated on random dummy inputs without `test` leakage)** on identical `smolvla_m7_deltaloop.pt` weights (`N = 400` official `test` episodes, `25,296` chunks) alongside 3-seed `{42, 43, 44}` verification (`Pooled N = 720`) establishes three nuanced conclusions:
1. **Intra-Checkpoint Truncation Sensitivity vs. Single-Pass `M5` Dominance (`N = 400` Matched `test` Eps):**
   - Within the multi-depth-trained `smolvla_m7_deltaloop.pt` checkpoint, closed-loop counts progress from `Fixed L=1` (`11/400 = 2.75%`) to `Fixed L=2` (`16/400 = 4.00%`, `H4b: two-sided p = 0.1652` NS), `Adaptive CRN` (`17/400 = 4.25%` at `1.11` hops, `H4d: two-sided p = 0.0696` NS; one-sided `p_1 = 0.0348`), and `Latent L2 Conv` (`18/400 = 4.50%` at `2.28` hops, `H4e: two-sided p = 0.0654` NS; one-sided `p_1 = 0.0327`).
   - Crucially, **none of the intra-checkpoint `M7` comparisons reach two-sided $p < 0.05$, and even `M7`'s highest point (`4.50%`) remains below dedicated single-pass 2-layer GDN `smolvla_m5_gdn2l_l1` (`21/400 = 5.25%`, `H4c: p = 0.4722` NS)**. Thus, on RoboMME, `M7`'s `L=1 \to L \ge 2` recovery reflects sensitivity of random-`L`-trained weights to `L=1` truncation rather than a net closed-loop advantage of multi-hop Coda looping over single-pass recurrent GDN readout.
2. **Why `Latent L2 Conv` Fails on Discrete Pointer Chains (Cycle 1) Yet Works on Continuous Visuomotor Manifolds (Cycle 3B):**
   - On symbolic $k$-hop entity chains (`Cycle 1 Task 1A-Retro`), intermediate basis jumps produce non-monotonic step norms under key superposition, causing L2-convergence halting to drop by `-25.4` to `-35.9` percentage points (`61.9–70.4%` vs `CRN` `95.8–97.8%`).
   - On `SmolVLA-DeltaLoop`'s continuous Coda manifold, the weight-tied `Recurrent Core` behaves as a **damped fixed-point operator** (`Bai et al., NeurIPS 2019`): `Chunk 0` post-demonstration shifts delay convergence (`Chunk-0 L_t = 2.653`), whereas settled execution chunks contract at $L=2$ (`Subsequent = 2.266`). Meanwhile, Stage-II `CRN` (`1.11` hops, `4.25%`) matches `Latent L2 Conv` (`4.50%`, `H5b: p = 0.8990` NS) at **`-51.3%` fewer recurrent hops** by directly predicting $L=1$ sufficiency.
3. **3-Seed Closed-Loop Audit (`seeds = {42, 43, 44}`, `Pooled N = 720`) & Holm-Bonferroni Multiplicity Control:**
   - Across `3` independent training seeds (`N = 240`/seed, `Pooled N = 720`), episode success counts (`s42 / s43 / s44`) are `SmolVLA-M0`: `3, 6, 7 / 240` (`2.22% \pm 0.87%`), `SmolVLA-M2` (`FrameSamp-32`): `14, 16, 25 / 240` (`7.64% \pm 2.44%`), and `SmolVLA-M7` (`DeltaLoop CRN`): `10, 10, 9 / 240` (`4.03% \pm 0.24%`, `L_t = 1.14 \pm 0.13`; note that with $n=3$ seeds, `M7`'s small sample SD `0.24 pt` lies below expected binomial sampling noise `~1.27 pt/seed` and does not establish structural variance reduction).
   - While `H_MS1` (`Pooled M7 vs M0`: `+1.81%`, `95% CI [+0.14%, +3.61%]`, two-sided `p = 0.0414` unadjusted) is nominally positive, **under family-wise Holm-Bonferroni correction across all `m = 38` Cycle 3B/4 hypothesis tests, only `4` tests unconditionally remain statistically significant: `H_MS2` (`M2 > M0`, `p <= 0.0002, p_holm <= 0.0076`), `H0b` (`M2 > M0`, `p = 0.0002, p_holm = 0.0074`), `H_MS3` (`M7 < M2` at `k=0`, `-3.61%`, `p = 0.0008, p_holm = 0.0288`), and `H1c` (`M5 > M0`, `p = 0.0012, p_holm = 0.0420`)**, whereas `H7d` (`M5` $k=3$ vs $k=0$ drop on `10` lost / `0` gained pairs) is **Holm-NS under primary exact two-sided McNemar (`p = 0.0020`, `p_holm = 0.0680`)** and Holm-significant only conditional on finite-sample bootstrap (`p <= 0.0002`, `p_holm <= 0.0074`) (while tied floor counts `8/180` on `Permanence` and `1/180` on `Counting` do not establish statistical equivalence without TOST bounds; under $k=3$ un-reset clutter, both `M2` [`14/240 \to 3/240`, `H7b p = 0.0032` unadj., `p_holm = 0.1056` NS] and single-pass `M5` [`12/240 \to 2/240`, `H7d` despite offline `cos = 0.992`] drop in closed loop, whereas `M7`'s smaller drop [`10/240 \to 7/240`, `H7c p = 0.4544` NS] yields non-significant head-to-head and DiD differences [`H7a p = 0.1950`, `H7f p = 0.0670`, `H7h p = 0.0976`, `H7i p = 0.1238` NS] at `< 6%` floor counts).
